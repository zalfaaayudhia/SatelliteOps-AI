Satellite Communication Troubleshooting

Communication status can be affected by link availability, ground station connectivity, antenna configuration, or onboard subsystem conditions.

When a communication issue is reported, record the affected link, observation time, recent status changes, and available error messages.

Review ground station status and available link telemetry before narrowing down the cause. If evidence is insufficient, document the uncertainty rather than assigning an unverified root cause.

All recovery actions must follow the mission's approved procedures and authorization requirements.
