# Satellite Incident Response

## Initial assessment
When an anomaly is reported, confirm the observation time, identify the affected subsystem, and review the available telemetry and recent events.

## Investigation workflow
1. Validate that the observation is present in the source data.
2. Compare the reading with documented limits and recent history when available.
3. Check for related alarms or subsystem communication issues.
4. Record findings and unresolved questions.
5. Escalate according to the approved mission procedure.

## Communication
Incident summaries should distinguish confirmed facts from hypotheses. Include evidence, impact if known, actions taken, and the next recommended investigation step.
